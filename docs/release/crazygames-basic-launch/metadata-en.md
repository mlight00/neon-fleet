Pick a weapon and drop straight into the fight — no menus, no sign-up. Grow your drone fleet, fuse weapons into resonance combos, beat sector bosses, and push a little farther every run.

## Detailed description
Neon Fleet is a vertical arcade shoot-'em-up roguelite. You steer a fleet of drones across neon sectors, weaving through enemy fire while your ships fire automatically. Collect crystals and supply pods to grow your fleet, pick up POW drops from mini-bosses to upgrade and combine weapons, and choose your route through each sector's branching map.

Every run is short and self-contained (about 5–15 minutes). Death is not the end: you keep your best score and permanent Hangar upgrades, and a single click restarts you with the same weapon so you can immediately try again. Clear all six sectors to liberate the galaxy and unlock Endless Expedition.

Core loop: **choose weapon → fight → grow → beat the sector boss → go deeper → die → upgrade → play again.**

## Controls (as actually supported)
- **Move:** move the mouse, or drag on touch — the fleet follows. Also **← →** or **A / D** on keyboard.
- **Fire:** automatic (always on).
- **Charge shot:** hold the left mouse button, hold **Space**, or hold the on-screen **⚡** button (mobile). Releasing fires a piercing charge beam.
- **Pause:** the on-screen **⏸** button, or **Esc**.
- No other keys are required. `Esc` is optional (it is not needed to play) so it will not fight portal full-screen behavior.

## Desktop / mobile support
- **Desktop (primary):** Chrome and Edge, mouse or keyboard. Fully supported.
- **Mobile (playable, not the primary target of this launch):** touch drag to move + **⚡** button to charge. The portrait canvas fits phone screens; landscape iframes are letterboxed (the portrait play-field is centered and scaled to fit — never cropped).

## Save method
Local browser storage (`localStorage`, key `neonFleet.v1`). Persists: best score / furthest sector, coins, permanent Hangar upgrades, unlocked weapons and systems, and sound settings. **No account, no server, no cloud save.** Clearing browser data resets progress.

## Known limitations
- Portrait-oriented play-field; on wide/short screens it is letterboxed (centered, never cropped).
- Progress is per-browser only (local storage) — it does not sync across devices.
- Audio is OGG (Chrome/Edge native). The portal build ships OGG only.
- Ads / monetization are **not** enabled in this Basic Launch build (by design).
