### ③ 3D 파이프라인이 남은 차이마저 지운다

## 2. 성장 설계 — 이번 재제작의 핵심

**단계마다 형태 언어를 하나씩 더합니다.** 크기만 키우면 지금과 같아집니다.

| 등급 | 이름 | 새로 생기는 것 | 캔버스 | 가로비 |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| T0 | EMBER | 날개 **없음** — 가는 창 하나 | **180×512** | 0.35 |
| T1 | FLARE | **날개 한 쌍**이 처음 돋는다 | **280×512** | 0.55 |
| T2 | ARCLIGHT | 날개가 **앞으로 꺾이고** 측면 포드 | **380×512** | 0.74 |
| T3 | AURORA | **동체가 둘로 갈라진다**(쌍동체, 가운데 뚫림) | **460×512** | 0.90 |
| T4 | ZENITH | 쌍동체 + **중앙 사령탑** | **520×512** | 1.02 |
| T5 | QUASAR | 날개가 **활짝 전개된 요새** | **660×512** | 1.29 |

**가로비가 0.35에서 1.29로 단조 증가합니다.** 이것이 "자란다"를 만드는 유일한 장치입니다.

⚠️T0을 지금(0.50)보다 **더 좁게** 잡았습니다. 시작이 빈약해야 성장이 큽니다.
⚠️T5는 세로보다 가로가 깁니다 — 화면을 꽉 채우는 위압감이 목적입니다.

## 4. 등급별 프롬프트

### T0 — EMBER

```
Top-down view of a small futuristic spacecraft, viewed from directly above, nose pointing up.
A single narrow blade-like hull with NO WINGS AT ALL — just a slim vertical spear of a ship.
Two small circular thruster nozzles at the bottom rear, glowing bright cyan.
White and silver armor panels with a single gold spine running down the center.
Very narrow and long proportions — the ship should look thin and fragile, like a scout probe.
Painted 3D render style, clean crisp edges, perfectly symmetrical left-right,
transparent background, no shadow, no ground.
```

```
Same design family as the attached ship, but EVOLVED: it has now grown ONE PAIR OF WINGS
sweeping backward from the middle of the hull — this is the first time wings appear.
The central spear hull remains but is slightly thicker.
Two thruster nozzles, larger than before, glowing cyan.
White and silver armor with gold accents along the wing roots.
Moderately narrow proportions. Painted 3D render style, symmetrical, transparent background.
```

```
Top-down view of a futuristic spacecraft, viewed from directly above, nose pointing up.
Same lineage as the attached ship, EVOLVED: the wings now SWEEP FORWARD instead of backward,

Wider and more aggressive than before. The forward-swept wing angle must be unmistakable.

Painted 3D render style, symmetrical, transparent background.
```

```
Top-down view of a futuristic spacecraft, viewed from directly above, nose pointing up.
Same lineage as the attached ship, but now a MAJOR TRANSFORMATION: the single hull has
```

```
Top-down view of a massive futuristic fortress warship, viewed from directly above, nose pointing up.

now framed by these deployed panels.
THREE enormous thruster nozzles across the rear, burning bright blue-white.
MUCH WIDER THAN TALL — it should look like it barely fits the screen.
White and silver armor with intense gold and cyan glowing edges along the deployed panels.
Painted 3D render style, symmetrical, transparent background.
```
