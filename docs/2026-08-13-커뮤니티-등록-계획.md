## 2. 게시 문안 (영문 — 그대로 복사)

### r/playmygame 용

**제목**

```
[pc] (web) Neon Fleet — a free browser shmup where your fleet grows by shooting math gates. No ads, no install.
```

**본문**

```
Play: https://mlight00.github.io/neon-fleet/

I built this because I kept seeing that "grow your fleet" minigame in mobile ads and wanted
to actually play it — without the fake fishing part. It's a free hobby project. No ads,
no monetisation, no install, no account.

What it is
- Move your drone formation left/right, shoot energy crystals and supply ships to grow it
- Warp gates apply +, x, -, ÷ to your fleet — pick the right lane
- Hold to charge a piercing lance that clears a vertical column
- Your flagship physically upgrades: hull, command frame, and weapon mounts all change
  shape with weapon type / level / evolution
- Zoom past 200% and it switches to a real 3D stern-chase view (Three.js) while the game
  logic stays 2D — the 2D simulation is the single source of truth, 3D is a read-only view

Tech (in case anyone cares)
- Vanilla JS + Canvas 2D, no build step, no framework
- Three.js only for the 3D chase view, lazy-loaded only when you zoom in
- ~1,100 automated tests, mostly execution-based rather than string matching

What I'd like feedback on
1. First 60 seconds — is it obvious what to do? Where do you get lost?
2. Does the fleet growth feel rewarding or grindy?
3. The 3D stern view — does it help or is it disorienting?
4. Difficulty curve: where did you die, and did it feel fair?

Controls: mouse or arrow keys, Space (or the ⚡ button on mobile) to charge.
Works on phones too.
```

⚠️**규칙**: 피드백 요청 항목(4개)을 반드시 남긴다. "그냥 해보세요" 식 글은 반응이 없고 규칙 위반이 되는 곳도 있다.

### itch.io 페이지 — 짧은 소개문

```
A free browser shmup about growing a drone fleet.

Shoot crystals and supply ships to add drones. Warp gates multiply or divide your fleet —
choose your lane. Hold to charge a piercing lance. Your flagship physically rebuilds itself
as you upgrade: hull, command frame, weapon mounts.

Zoom in past 200% and the game switches to a real 3D stern-chase camera while the underlying
2D simulation keeps running unchanged.

No ads. No monetisation. No install. Hobby project.
```

**태그**: `shmup` `shoot-em-up` `html5` `browser` `arcade` `space` `3d` `free`

## 4. 피드백을 받은 뒤

들어온 피드백을 이 파일에 쌓는다.

```text
docs/feedback/2026-08-__-<출처>.md
```

분류 기준:

| 분류 | 처리 |
|---|---|
| **버그** | 재현 → 수정 → 테스트 추가 (지금까지 방식 그대로) |
| **난이도·밸런스** | ⚠️게임 truth 변경이므로 **이사님 승인 후**에만 |
| **조작·UI 혼란** | 대개 즉시 고칠 수 있다 |
| **취향 문제** | 여러 명이 같은 말을 할 때만 움직인다 |

⚠️**한 명의 의견으로 게임을 바꾸지 않는다.** 같은 지적이 3명 이상에게서 나오면 그때 신호로 본다.
